# Code of Conduct

DCRecomp is a community of people who like old games and hard technical problems. Newcomers are welcome.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good faith, and critique work rather than people. Disagreements are settled with evidence.

**Not accepted:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or pressuring people
to share copyrighted game data.

**Reporting:** use GitHub's "Report abuse" option or contact a DCRecomp organization owner privately. Reports are handled
confidentially.

**Enforcement:** organization owners may edit or remove content and block participants who break these rules.
